Privacy Policy

This app is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel. It reads the channel's own analytics and public data about selected YouTube channels through Google's APIs. The data is used only to create a weekly summary report, sent to the owner's own Discord or Telegram account. Data is not sold or shared with third parties, except that Google's Gemini service processes the text to write the report. Access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
